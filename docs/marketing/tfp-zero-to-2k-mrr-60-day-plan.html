01

Executive Decision

The direct answer before the detailed plan.

OK
Launch as a web-first creator marketplace, not an app-install campaign.

The current product is an Astro SSR web app with strong public routes. With Rs.5,000/month, sending paid traffic to specific web pages is more practical than paying for app installs. Build native app and AdMob only after you prove contest retention and paid conversion.

Best first customer

Photographers who need models, models who need portfolio shoots, and small creative academies/studios that can sponsor a challenge.

Best first monetization

Subscriptions, sponsored contests, featured listings, verification/profile boosts, and event/opportunity promotion.

Worst first monetization

Forcing users to watch ads before they can participate. It adds friction, creates policy risk, and will not reach $2k/month quickly.

Creator/community incentives Rs.1,000 Rs.500 Small prize, feature, certificate, or sponsored boost credits. Avoid cash claims unless legal/prize terms are ready.
AD
Do not run Google App Campaigns at this budget.

Google recommends daily app-campaign budgets at 50x target CPI or 10x target CPA. Rs.5,000/month is too small for app-install learning. Use web traffic and exact search until retention and paid conversion are proven.

Meta campaign setup

  1. Create one campaign named TFP Contest Launch - India - Web Traffic.
  2. Objective: traffic or leads. Use traffic first if no conversion events exist; switch to leads/conversions after events are reliable.
  3. Daily budget: Rs.100/day for 30 days, or Rs.150/day for 20 stronger days. Do not split into many ad sets.
  4. Ad set 1: India top creative cities: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Kochi.
  5. Age: 18-34 first; exclude under-18 because contest terms are 18+.
  6. Interests: photography, portrait photography, fashion photography, modeling, makeup artist, camera, Lightroom, Photoshop.
  7. Placements: Instagram Reels, Stories, Feed; Facebook Feed only if CPC is low.
  8. Landing page: specific contest page or opportunities page with UTM, not homepage.
  9. Kill rule after 72 hours: pause creative if CTR below 1%, CPC above Rs.10, or signup rate below 5%.

Copy: "Enter a skill-based photo challenge. Upload one portrait, get seen by local creators, and build your TFP profile."

CTA: Enter free contest

Click: Web contest detail page, e.g. /en-IN/contests/<slug>

Ad 2: Model portfolio

Visual: split screen: model profile, portfolio grid, opportunity card.

Copy: "Find portfolio-building TFP shoots. Create a profile, discover photographers, and apply to verified opportunities."

CTA: Find shoots

Click: /en-IN/opportunities with UTM

Ad 3: Sponsor pitch

Visual: contest card with sponsor logo placeholder and submission gallery.

Copy: "Sponsor a themed photo challenge for photographers and models. Get brand placement, entries, and community reach."

CTA: Sponsor a challenge

Click: new /sponsor-a-contest or /contact?intent=sponsor-contest

Creative rules

  • Show the actual app surface: contest card, profile, submission gallery, opportunities list.
  • Use one promise per ad. Do not say "earn money" unless payments/prizes/legal terms are live and compliant.
  • Put "18+ skill-based contest" in contest ads when prizes are involved.
  • Every prize ad must show prize value, eligibility, start/end date, and link to official contest terms.
  • For Meta/Instagram promotions, include platform release language in the contest terms and ad landing page.

07

"Watch an Ad to Participate in Contest" Recommendation

Short answer: do not use this as the launch mechanic.

NO
Do not make ad-watching mandatory for Phase 1 contest entry.

Your own contest terms say Phase 1 entry is free and skill-based. Mandatory rewarded ads add friction, create policy and legal complexity, and distract from the real revenue levers: subscriptions, boosts, and sponsors.

Idea Use now? Safe version Unsafe version
Watch ad to enter contest No If tested later, make it optional, clearly disclosed, skip/dismiss allowed, with a free alternative entry route. Blocking entry unless ad is watched, especially where prize is cash/gift card/physical reward.
Watch ad for profile boost credit Later Non-transferable in-platform visibility credit with frequency caps and no cash value. Promising money, gift cards, or transferable rewards for ads.
Watch ad for extra contest submission No Not recommended because repository rules enforce one submission per user per contest. Breaking the one-submission rule and damaging fairness.
Watch ad after submission Maybe Optional "support the platform" style placement is risky if it nudges the user. Prefer normal display/sponsor ads. Misleading users that watching improves judging odds.

If you later build rewarded ads

  • Build this in app only after a native Android/iOS app exists, because AdMob rewarded ads are app-oriented.
  • Use server-side verification and store one reward event per user/action.
  • Reward must be non-transferable and usable only inside TFP.
  • Show clear text before the ad: "Watch one ad to receive one profile boost credit. This does not affect judging or prize odds."
  • Frequency cap: max 2 rewarded ads per user/day and max 10/month at first.
  • Do not use rewarded ads in flows with cash/gift-card/real-money prizes until legal review is complete.

60-Day Roadmap After Go-Live

Every week has product, marketing, sales, and measurement steps.

Week 1

Launch foundation

  • Ship pricing page, sponsor inquiry page, and conversion analytics events.
  • Create one flagship free skill contest with clear rules, judging criteria, 18+ eligibility, and no misleading prize claims.
  • Manually recruit 50 photographers, 50 models, 10 makeup artists/stylists, and 5 sponsors from local communities.
  • Start Instagram content: 2 Reels/day, 1 carousel/day, 10 story replies/day.
Week 2

First paid signal test

  • Run Rs.100/day Meta traffic to the contest page for 7 days.
  • Test two creatives: photographer contest and model opportunities.
  • DM 20 photography academies/camera stores/studios with sponsor offer.
  • Publish 5 SEO pages: "TFP shoots in Bengaluru", "TFP shoots in Mumbai", "Photography contests India", "Models for portfolio shoot", "Photographer collaboration India".
Week 3

Activation and referrals

  • Push every new user to complete profile, upload 3 images, and share referral link on WhatsApp.
  • Give manual "Founding Creator" badge to high-quality early users.
  • Add founder-led onboarding calls for first 20 serious photographers/models.
  • Pause any paid creative above Rs.10 CPC or below 5% signup conversion.
Week 4

First revenue push

  • Sell 5 sponsored contest/event packs at Rs.4,999 each.
  • Offer early PRO at Rs.399/month for first 100 paying users, then move to Rs.499.
  • Launch featured profile/listing for Rs.299/week.
  • Publish winner/submission showcase and tag every participant.
Week 5

Retargeting and sponsor proof

  • Start Rs.50/day retargeting to visitors who did not register or submit.
  • Create sponsor proof page: reach, signups, submissions, top cities, examples.
  • Contact 50 sponsors with proof and offer two packages: Rs.4,999 starter, Rs.9,999 featured.
  • Launch second contest with sponsor/category theme.
Week 6

Subscription conversion

  • Add upgrade prompts where users hit opportunity/event quota or want featured placement.
  • Publish creator analytics preview to make PRO tangible.
  • Email/WhatsApp all active users: "Unlock more listings + visibility this month".
  • Run exact-match Google Search for Rs.1,000/month only on high-intent keywords.
Week 7

Country expansion test

  • Review analytics by country. If India is still dominant, keep paid ads India-only.
  • If organic signups exist in Nepal/Sri Lanka/Bangladesh, create no-prize portfolio challenge pages for those markets.
  • Recruit one local ambassador per promising country before paid ads.
  • Do not run cash/prize ads outside India without local terms review.
Week 8

Scale what converted

  • Keep only the top ad creative and top two cities by signup-to-submission conversion.
  • Close enough sponsors/subscribers to reach the Rs.190k/month run-rate target.
  • Convert manual founder sales scripts into repeatable CRM pipeline.
  • Decide next build: native app/rewarded ads only if retention is strong; otherwise continue web revenue features.

Metrics and Stop Rules

Numbers to watch every day.

Metric Good Warning Action
Meta CPC Under Rs.6 Above Rs.10 Pause creative, test new first 2 seconds of video, tighten landing page.
Landing signup rate 8%+ Under 5% Change CTA from generic register to "Enter free contest" or "Find shoots".
Signup to profile completion 50%+ Under 30% Simplify onboarding; show profile completion checklist.
Signup to first upload/submission 20%+ Under 10% Make upload/submission CTA visible on contest and profile pages.
Referral shares per active creator 2+ Under 0.5 Show WhatsApp referral CTA after profile completion and submission.
Paid conversion 2%-4% active users Under 1% Improve PRO value: analytics, boosts, limits, sponsor visibility.
Sponsor close rate 10%+ of warm outreach Under 4% Lower first package to Rs.2,999 or add stronger proof/reporting.
